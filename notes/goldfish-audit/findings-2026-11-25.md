# Blind-reading audit, 2026-11-25: findings

Third run, forge-2.0.16 (8,497 census cards), 7 Sonnet readers, ~780K tokens.
Population: 1,501 cards in 7 batches (215, 215, 215, 214, 214, 214, 214). The census was built from the current code (the 0.3.9 fixes plus the second audit's mana and draw fixes). The 2026-11-23 run had 140 disagreements (61 mana, 79 draw) and 52 more in left_out only.

- **168 cards differ from the census.** 60 of them differ only in left_out and were not triaged.
- **Mana side, all 64 triaged:** 4 real, 14 accepted simplifications, 46 agent. All 4 real ones affect a flag or a fidelity note only; no card's mana units are wrong.
- **Draw side, all 44 triaged, ranked most played first:** 12 real, 1 simplification, 31 agent.
  - Of the 20 most played, 4 are real (20%).
  - Across all 44, 27% are real. Six of the twelve are already in TODO.md (Worth doing).
- **Most agent disagreements repeat last run's.** The 2026-11-23 rulings were never added to brief.md (the TODO.md item "Add the second audit's rulings"). They would have covered about 30 of the 46 mana-side agent disagreements.

## Real, mana side (4)

1. **Station Planets and Spacecraft get the wrong note.** Evendo, Waking Haven (848) and Uthros, Titanic Godcore (1184) say "mana it gives other permanents left out". The Eternity Elevator (1434), outside the disagreements, is the same.
   - Cause: `gives_others_mana` (forge_profile.rs ~1337) counts `Affected$ Card.counters_GE12_CHARGE` as others, even with `AffectedDefined$ Self`.
   - Effect: the fidelity text only. undercounted is correctly false since 0.3.9.
2. **Mana given to other creatures doesn't set undercounted on a rock.** Esika, God of the Tree (2506) gives other legends, often the commander, "{T}: any colour". Orochi Merge-Keeper is the same.
   - Cause: `gives_others_mana` adds the note but never sets the flag, although the `Profile::undercounted` doc says it covers this.
   - Chromatic Lantern only gives colours, so false is right for it.
3. **A fixed amount under a condition (`Count$Compare`) is read as mana that grows with the board.** Ugin's Labyrinth (2726) makes C, or CC with a card imprinted.
   - Ilysian Caryatid (1741), Leafkin Druid, Raucous Audience and Tower Worker take the same path.
   - Cause: in `produced()`, any `Amount$` svar becomes "grows, counted as one, undercounted". Only the flag and note are wrong.

New, 6:

- **The monarch is never read as a draw.** Court of Grace (1630). Also every other Court (Garenbrig 2366, Ambition 2580, Cunning 2802, …), Queen Marchesa (2746), Regal Behemoth and Palace Jailer. In a goldfish nobody takes the crown, so these should be end-step draw engines.
- **A trigger the card sets off itself isn't traced.** Call of the Ring (1432): its upkeep `RingTemptsYou` sets off its own draw. Fathom Mage (1715, borderline): Evolve puts counters on it, which sets off `CounterAdded`.
- **An inline `Remembered$Amount/Plus.1` draw count.** Brass's Tunnel-Grinder (1621) reads draw 2 where the net is 1. `amount()` falls back to VARIABLE_DRAW (3), and the AnyNumber discard counts 1.
- **A draw ability that gives the card to an opponent is read as repeatable.** Humble Defector (1789) reads as a draw engine; its `GainControl Defined$ Self` makes it a one-time draw 2.
- **A self-sacrifice-for-mana card that then draws is not read.** Chromatic Star (2841) reads other; the brief makes it draw 1. Nihil Spellbomb may be the same (not checked).

## Rulings for brief.md

What the triage suggests adding to `brief.md`. The first two repeat the 2026-11-23 findings.

- Apply the 2026-11-23 rulings that are still pending in TODO.md. They would have avoided about 30 mana-side and 15 draw-side agent disagreements: with_lands.makes includes the base kinds (Nykthos, Three Tree City, Cabal Stronghold, Baldur's Gate); a mana ability counts only if its cost is tapping the card, mana, or life once a turn, so abilities that tap or sacrifice other permanents, energy, forage, graveyard lands, no-tap life and power 0 don't count (17 cards); lands put only into hand are not ramp; gated, repeating and per-opponent searches don't count; max speed, luck counters and Selvala's parley don't count; Dryad Arbor enters tapped; granted mana on a creature of the kind it grants to is a rock, undercounted; revealing a creature type uses the snarl form; per-turn card advantage without 'draw' is other; cycling is draw 1; draw triggers that wait on the game are not engines.
- Decide exile-to-play (7 disagreements: Jeska's Will, Light Up the Stage, Genesis Wave, Glimpse the Impossible, Aminatou's Augury, Inspired Tinkering, Escape to the Wilds), then write the decision into both the brief and the code.
- undercounted is about the amount of mana. Colours given to lands (Chromatic Lantern) are not undercounted; extra mana given to creatures (Esika) is.
- 'Search for up to N' is N, not X (Reshape the Earth: 10).
- Restricted mana counts like any other even when it is the ability making the most (Eldrazi Temple: CC).
- not_played follows roles even for a card with no mana cost (Sol Talisman: Ramp).
- A rock with an X-only cost takes its whole cost at X = 1 as extra (Astral Cornucopia {X}{X}{X}: 3).
- A refilling wheel (Windfall) is {"refill": 3}: add the form to the schema.
- An ETB draw paid by sacrificing another creature is no draw (Disciple of Bolas, Disciple of Freyalise).
- 'Choose a player to draw' (Gluntch) is no draw. Write draw 0 as other.
- Wrenn and Seven's +1 (lands from the top four into hand) needs a ruling: draw engine, as the census reads it, or other under the Coiling Oracle rule.
- Station is never done, so 3+ and 12+ abilities are absent (Uthros Research Craft, the Planets).
- Draw size for multiple cascades or takes (Call Forth the Tempest, Stock Up, Dig Through Time). Also: repeatable library-to-battlefield land abilities (Shigeki, Fertilid) are lands from the library's top, so they are other with Ramp not played.
