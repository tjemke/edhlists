# Blind-reading audit, 2026-11-23: triage

1,500 cards in 7 batches, compared with the census by compare.py and split.py. The per-batch reports are in `~/goldfish-audit/batches/out*.jsonl.cmp` and `out*.split` (outside the repo, and gone with this VM).
Census: forge-2.0.16, 8,497 cards. The 2026-11-22 run (0.3.6) had 250 disagreements: 99 mana, 98 draw.

- **140** cards differ from the census: 61 on the mana side, 79 on the draw side.
- **52** more differ only in left_out. I didn't triage those, since the brief says left_out matters least.
- **Mana side** (all 61 triaged): 7 real, 11 accepted simplifications, 43 agent.
- **Draw side**: 16 of the 79 are only a format slip. Batch 4 wrote "effect": n instead of {"draw": n}, and the values agree (Arcane Denial, Night's Whisper, Preordain, ...). I triaged the 30 most played of the other 63: 13 real (43%) and 17 agent. That puts about 27 of the 63 as real, or about 34% of all 79.

## Real, mana side

1. **Station planets get the "mana it gives other permanents" flag** (`undercounted` and the note), though they give no mana. Examples: Adagia (1495), Susur Secundi (1702).
   - In `mana_lines`, `starts_with("counters_GE1")` also matches `counters_GE12_CHARGE`.
   - `line.granted` sets the flag before the line is checked to be `AB$ Mana`.
   - This probably also explains TODO's Queen's Diamond. It affects only fidelity and doubts.
2. **Mana granted to creatures of a subtype doesn't count the card itself.** Manaweft Sliver (3316) reads as other with Ramp not played; Katilda (4603) is the same. `fits` accepts only the YouCtrl/Self/HasCounters properties, so `Creature.Sliver+YouCtrl` fails. Gemhide Sliver (`Affected$ Sliver`) is read correctly.
3. **A land search paid with the card's own counters isn't land ramp.** Khalni Heart Expedition (2806), Fertilid (3246). `land_ramp` requires `!cost.other`, and `SubCounter` sets it.
4. **A cost reducer on a Class level is missed.** Artist's Talent (1542), Fortune Teller's Talent (3857). `reduces_costs` reads only `S:` lines, not the static that `K:Class` adds.
5. **Jeweled Amulet (3493)** is Unreadable, and the oracle fallback reads it as a lasting any-colour rock. It only stores one mana and nets none.

## Real, draw side (13 of the 30 sampled)

1. **An opponent's step read as the deck's own.**
   - Archon of Cruelty (951): the opponent's edict sets `sacrifices_another`, which zeroes the draw.
   - Loran of the Third Path (337): `for_you` rejects `ValidTgts` Opponent even with `Defined$ TargetedAndYou`.
2. **The Draw-role oracle fallback gives draw 1 where the cards aren't the deck's, or need combat.** Tibalt's Trickery (601), Hunter's Insight (1079).
3. **Linked or derived variable counts each read as a fresh 3.** Only a bare `Count$xPaid` gets 2 in `amount()`.
   - Valakut Awakening (517) reads 0; it should be 1.
   - Hydroid Krasis (1560) and Wan Shi Tong (1186) read 3, where half of X = 2 gives 1.
   - Windfall (162) reads 0.
4. **Draws the reader doesn't walk.**
   - Saga chapters: Summon: Bahamut (1164), and The Legend of Kyoshi (2895, outside the sample).
   - `Defined$ Remembered` after a ChooseNumber: Wheel of Misfortune (1241) gets 1 instead of 4.
   - A sacrifice in a trigger's `Cost$ Sac<1/Creature.Other>`: Sephiroth (1199) reads draw 1.
5. **A conditional draw on the entering creature's name.** Guardian Project (320). In singleton the condition is almost always met.
6. **Already in TODO.md (Worth doing):** Fact or Fiction (699), Combustible Gearhulk (1028).

## Brief rulings

Rulings for `brief.md` that would have avoided the agent disagreements (not applied yet; the
last two need a decision):

- A draw effect is always {"draw": n}. Batch 4 wrote a bare number 16 times ("effect": 2), which accounts for 16 of the 79 draw-side disagreements.
- with_lands.makes is the whole of what the land makes once the lands are out, so it includes the base ability's kinds (Nykthos: makes ["C"], with_lands {lands 3, makes ["WUBRGC"]}). This avoids Nykthos, Three Tree City, Cabal Stronghold and Baldur's Gate.
- A mana ability counts only if its cost is tapping the card, mana, or life once a turn. Ignore abilities that tap or sacrifice other permanents (Springleaf Drum, Phyrexian Altar and Tower, the creature-tap lands), or that cost energy, forage, graveyard cards, counters on itself, unlimited life (Treasonous Ogre), or nothing at all (Vivi). This avoids 14 disagreements.
- Lands put only into hand (Liliana of the Dark Realms, Archaeomancer's Map, Vorinclex) aren't land ramp: use other, with Ramp not played.
- A repeating land-search trigger (Karametra) isn't land ramp. A search gated on an opponent having more lands never happens. Lands found per opponent (Tempt with Discovery) count none. Kicker is read unkicked, and a larger count under a condition (Entish Restoration) isn't taken.
- Max speed is never reached. An opening-hand counter (Gemstone Caverns) isn't there. Mana from revealed cards (Selvala) isn't a rock. Dryad Arbor enters tapped (summoning sick).
- A creature that gives a mana ability to creatures of a kind it is itself is a rock, with undercounted true when others get it too (Rishkar, Gemhide Sliver, Inga and Esika). Revealing a creature type to enter untapped (Temple of the Dragon Queen) uses the snarl form, 'untapped if you reveal'.
- Exile-to-play on a Draw-role spell (Jeska's Will, Light Up the Stage, Genesis Wave, Glimpse the Impossible) is a conflict between two rulings. The census uses 'card advantage without draw → 1'; the readers use 'exile to play is not a draw'. Pick one.
- A permanent's per-turn card advantage that doesn't say 'draw' (Bolas's Citadel, Ripples of Undeath, Chimil's discover) is other. A draw ability usable every turn is an engine even when Ramp comes first for not_played (Black Market Connections, Gleaming Splendor). Cycling on a spell is draw 1. A Class level-up draw isn't counted. An extra land drop is left_out engine.
- Say whether half X is 1 (X = 2) or 3 (variable). Say whether Windfall nets 0 or counts like Wheel of Fortune. Also rule on: spree or kicker extra, wheels that depend on hand size, modal and cascade draws, fetch lands that enter tapped (Mountain Valley), and left_out for per-turn Treasure makers.
